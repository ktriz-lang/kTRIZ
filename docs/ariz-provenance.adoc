= ARIZ: why it is not modelled (yet)
:toc:

kTRIZ deliberately does not model ARIZ (the Algorithm of Inventive Problem
Solving). This page records the source check behind that decision (2026-10-23),
in the style of the other provenance documents.

== Finding

* Only the *part level* of ARIZ-85C (nine parts in three blocks: reformulate
  the problem, remove the contradiction, analyse the solution) is attested by
  two sources that agree: the MATRIZ wiki and Marconi's 1998 TRIZ Journal
  article.
* On the *step level* the two sources already disagree within parts 1 to 3
  (part 1: 7 steps vs. 6; part 3: 3 steps vs. 6), and part 2 is titled
  differently. There is no majority to reconcile against, so a step catalogue
  would be invented precision.
* *Licence* of the MATRIZ text is contradictory: "open access ... Creative
  Commons" (variant not named) next to "All Rights Reserved". That is not
  clear enough for Apache-2.0 redistribution. The Marconi page carries no
  licence notice.
* A nine-value enum of parts would add little and could suggest an authority
  kTRIZ cannot back.

== Limits of this check

The MATRIZ PDF of ARIZ-85C could not be read, the MATRIZ template page was
read only up to part 3, and neither the original text (Petrov/Zusman/
Altshuller/Zlotin) nor altshuller.ru was checked.

== What would change the decision

A written statement from MATRIZ naming the exact Creative Commons variant, or
a second freely licensed source with a matching step structure.
