= Laws of technical systems evolution data provenance

Provenance record for `EvolutionLaw` (eight classical laws) and
`EvolutionLawGroup` (statics, kinematics, dynamics). See `NOTICE` for the short
attribution summary.

Retrieved and evaluated: 2026-10-23.

== What is (and is not) bundled

kTRIZ bundles the eight classical laws: id, English label, group and a short
English description written independently for kTRIZ.

kTRIZ does NOT bundle evolution lines, patterns or TESE trends, worked
examples, full texts, German labels, or a DSL for applying the laws.

== Structure comparison

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Group | Laws

| Statics | Completeness of parts; energy conductivity; harmonization of rhythms
| Kinematics | Increasing ideality; uneven development of parts; transition to the supersystem
| Dynamics | Transition from macro to micro level; increasing substance-field involvement
|===

Both sources agree on this grouping. Naming differences (rhythm/rhythms,
super-system/supersystem) are translation variants. The group names and law
names are facts about the structure of the field; the descriptions in kTRIZ are
formulated from the meaning of each law, not paraphrased from either source.

== Divergence

MATRIZ lists a ninth law, "increase of dynamics". Only one source does, so it
is not an enum value and no majority decision is made. It is recorded here only.

== Rejected candidates

* The 11 patterns (Wikipedia) and the 11 master trends (MATRIZ): count and
  naming have no majority across sources, so bundling them would be invented
  precision.
* Material by Souchkov / CTQ Media: copyright.
* MATRIZ wiki sub-pages: contradictory licence statements (as with ARIZ, see
  link:ariz-provenance.adoc[docs/ariz-provenance.adoc]).
* German labels: no verified source.
* Wikipedia description texts: ShareAlike.

== Limits of this check

Not checked: altshuller.ru, the Petrov PDF, Altshuller's original books. The
result is a structural attestation from two secondary sources, not a primary
source verification. The licence wording above was read from the live pages on
the retrieval date.
